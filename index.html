About QCO

Building knowledge through competition

Quiz Competition Organization (QCO) is a student-run organization focused on quizzes, general knowledge and intellectual development.

QCO aims to create opportunities for students to participate in competitions, improve their knowledge, develop confidence and discover leadership potential.

Our goal is to make learning more engaging, competitive and meaningful for students.
