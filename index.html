About QCO

Knowledge. Competition. Achievement.

QCO — Quiz Competition Organization is an educational quiz competition organization focused on creating opportunities for students to learn, compete and achieve.

Through quiz competitions and knowledge-based activities, QCO aims to encourage learning, confidence and a healthy competitive spirit.

  • ✓ Knowledge-based competitions
  • ✓ Student-focused activities
  • ✓ Fair and healthy competition
  • ✓ Recognition of talented participants
