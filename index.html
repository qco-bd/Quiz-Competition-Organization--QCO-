ABOUT US

Who We Are

Quiz Competition Organization (QCO) is a student-led knowledge and quiz organization created to encourage learning, healthy competition and leadership among young people.

Building Knowledge Through Competition

QCO provides students with opportunities to test their knowledge, participate in quiz competitions and develop confidence through meaningful educational activities.

Our goal is not only to organize quizzes, but also to create an environment where students can learn, compete and grow.
