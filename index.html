ABOUT US

A platform built around knowledge.

QCO brings together students who believe that learning becomes more meaningful when knowledge is tested, shared and celebrated.

01

What is QCO?

Quiz Competition Organization (QCO) is a student-led knowledge and quiz organization created to encourage learning, healthy competition and leadership among young people.

We aim to create an environment where students can learn, compete and grow through meaningful educational activities.
