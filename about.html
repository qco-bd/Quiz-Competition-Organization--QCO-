Who We Are

Quiz Competition Organization (QCO) is an educational organization focused on knowledge, quiz competitions and student development.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage students to learn, think critically and participate in healthy academic competitions.

Our Vision

We aim to create a platform where students can demonstrate their knowledge, develop confidence and compete with talented students.
