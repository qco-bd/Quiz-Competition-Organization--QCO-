About QCO

Quiz Competition Organization (QCO) is an educational organization dedicated to developing knowledge, creativity, critical thinking and competitive skills among students.

Who We Are

Quiz Competition Organization (QCO) is an educational organization created to provide students with opportunities to test and improve their knowledge through quizzes, competitions, challenges and educational activities.

QCO aims to build a strong culture of learning, curiosity and healthy competition among students. Through both online and offline activities, QCO seeks to connect talented and enthusiastic learners from different schools, colleges and communities.

Our activities are designed not only to measure knowledge, but also to encourage students to learn beyond textbooks, think critically and develop confidence.

🎯

Our Mission

To create meaningful educational competitions that inspire students to learn more, think better and confidently demonstrate their knowledge and skills.

🌍

Our Vision

To build a nationwide platform where students can participate in quality competitions, discover their potential and connect with other young learners.

What We Do

QCO organizes educational competitions and knowledge based activities for students. Our activities may include general knowledge quizzes, science competitions, academic challenges and other learning based events.
